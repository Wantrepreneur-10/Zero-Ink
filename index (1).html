No rolls to buy

Thermal paper costs money, jams at the worst moment, and runs out mid-rush. Digital receipts cost you nothing per print.

Nothing to install

Fewer return arguments

Customers stop losing receipts, so refunds and exchanges stop turning into a debate about what they actually paid.

Three steps, once

01

Your customer gets a code

They sign up once and keep the same four digits forever — like a PIN they actually remember. Nothing to scan, no app to download.

02

You type it at checkout

Tap or say the items, punch in their four digits, hit send. It takes about as long as tearing off a paper receipt.

Questions restaurants ask

Do I need new hardware or a new till?

No. Zero Ink runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop you already have. It sits next to your current till and doesn't replace it.

What does the customer have to do?

Sign up once on their phone, which takes under a minute, and remember four digits. There's no app store download. They can add Zero Ink to their home screen if they want it one tap away.

What if a customer doesn't have a code?

Print them a paper receipt as usual. Zero Ink works alongside your printer, so nobody is left without one. A counter card with a QR code lets curious customers sign up while they wait.

Does it take payments?

No. Zero Ink only delivers the receipt. You keep taking payment exactly the way you do today.

Who can see a receipt?

Only the customer it was sent to and the business that sent it. The business never sees the customer's email, just their code and first name.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the pilot. We're working with our first restaurants and want your feedback more than your money.
