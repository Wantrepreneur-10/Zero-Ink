Built for the counter, not the boardroom

Zero Ink sits alongside whatever till you already use. It doesn't replace your system or your records — it just gets the customer their copy.

No rolls to buy

Thermal paper costs money, jams at the worst moment, and runs out mid-rush. Digital receipts cost you nothing per print.

Nothing to install

Open a web page on the tablet or phone already behind your counter. No new hardware, no integration project, no IT visit.

Fewer return arguments

Customers stop losing receipts, so refunds and exchanges stop turning into a debate about what they actually paid.

Three steps, once

01

Your customer gets a code

They sign up once and keep the same four digits forever — like a PIN they actually remember. Nothing to scan, no app to download.

02

You type it at checkout

Add the items, punch in their four digits, hit send. It takes about as long as tearing off a paper receipt.

03

It lands on their phone

Itemised and timestamped, in under a second. They never gave you their email, and you never had to ask for it.

For the person paying

Zero Ink is free for customers, and always will be.

  • Every receipt in one place. Searchable, not crumpled in a coat pocket.
  • Your email stays yours. The shop only ever sees four digits — not your name, not your inbox.
  • Nothing to install. Open the link, add it to your home screen if you like.

Where this is today

Zero Ink is in early pilot. It's working end to end — real codes, real receipts, delivered live — and it's free while we run it with our first restaurants.

If you run a place and want to try it, use it for a week and tell us what breaks. That feedback is the whole point right now.
